1.1 Research questions

We were asked to formulate a Descriptive and Explanatory research question. To aid in answering these research questions, we were asked to find 2 research papers relevant to the subject at hand. After much deliberating and thinking on what I would like to research (while fitting a social network analysys perspective), I have the following research questions currently in mind:

Descriptive question: Do MPs elected in cabinet have a higher in-degree out-degree popularity than non-elected MPs?

Explanatory question: Does inter-party interaction on Twitter influence party co-voting in motions?
