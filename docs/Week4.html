Definition of transitivity is: hoeveel triads die gesloten zijn / (aantal dat gesloten zijn + gesloten kunnen worden) Why do you have to do * 3? Because a triad can be closed for three egos. This is different than with local transitivity, which measures transitivity on an ego-level, how connected are the people I am connected with (to eachother). Ego can have higher local transitivity.

Verder nadenken: Global repricocity werkt hetzelfde (bij directed kan dat alleen, is wel allemaal moeilijker), waarbij je het *2 moet doen i.p.v. 3.

4 Webscraping

Webscraping, in essence, is gathering/fishing/scraping information from internet. This can be done automatically or manually. Webscraping is not the same as Data-sharing also exists, where people can give their Whatsapp information for instance (who did you message, not content of message)

Benefits:

  • Actual observed behaviour.

  • Cheap for large-scale research.

  • Time/geo stamped.

Limitations:

  • More complex analysis (more in-depth data, more difficult it is to analyze)

  • Selectivity of posts

  • Sampling

Ethics:

  • Is it allowed by the author/owner of data?

  • Is it allowed by the law?

  • Is it allowed by myself? Do I find it ethical?

  • Is it allowed by the ethical commission of your research institute

APIs

Aplication Programming Interface: Websites that allow/are designed for webscraping.
